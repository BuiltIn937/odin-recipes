Ribeye Steak

Easy recipe for a juicy ribeye steak using a cast iron skillet

Ingredients

  • 8 Oz. Ribeye Steak
  • 1 Stick of butter
  • Garlic Cloves
  • Grapeseed Oil
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Rosemary

Steps

  1. Remove the steaks from the fridge 30 minutes to an hour before cooking
  2. Pat the steaks dry with a paper towel
  3. Heat the cast iron skillet until it is lightly smoking
  4. Place the ribeyes in the cast iron skillet and let sear untouched for 4 minutes on both sides
